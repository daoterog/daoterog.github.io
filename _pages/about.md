I am a Master's student in Artificial Intelligence at the **University of Amsterdam**, with a deep interest in **computer vision**, **video understanding**, and **self-supervised learning**. I am particularly drawn to questions around learning robust representations and generalizing beyond labeled data.

Over the past year I have had the chance to present at top machine learning venues, contribute to workshops, and work under the guidance of incredible mentors. My long-term goal is to pursue research in academia — and I am currently looking to collaborate, learn, and contribute wherever I can bring value.

Before Amsterdam, I earned a Bachelor's in Mathematical Engineering from **Universidad EAFIT** in Medellín, Colombia, and spent two and a half years as an AI/ML Engineer building computer vision and agentic systems that had to survive real-world data. You can find the details on my [CV](/cv/).

Research interests
======
* Self-supervised learning
* Video and temporal representation learning
* Out-of-distribution generalization
* Research with social impact and accessibility
