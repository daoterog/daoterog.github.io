I am a Master's student in Artificial Intelligence at the **University of Amsterdam** with a deep interest in self-supervised and generative learning. I’m particularly drawn to questions around learning robust representations with the right geometry and generalizing beyond labeled data.


Research interests
======
* Generative modelling — flow matching, flow maps, and continuous formulations of discrete data
* Constrained optimization and the geometry of learning
* Self-supervised and label-efficient representation learning
* Sequence and temporal data, in speech and video
* Out-of-distribution generalization and robustness
* Research with social impact and accessibility
