I am a Master's student in Artificial Intelligence at the **University of Amsterdam**, supported by the Colfuturo Scholarship (ranked 4th out of 1000+ applicants). Before that I earned a Bachelor's in Mathematical Engineering from **Universidad EAFIT** in Medellín, Colombia.

My work sits between research and production. On the research side, I am interested in **self-supervised learning**, **anomaly detection under strong distribution imbalance**, and **constrained optimization** for machine learning. On the engineering side, I spent two and a half years as an AI/ML Engineer building and deploying computer vision and agentic systems that had to survive real-world data.

Research
======
* **Université de Montréal (Mila)** — *Oct. 2025 – Present.* Working on **Feasible Learning**, a per-sample constrained optimization framework that guarantees a minimum performance threshold on every training sample, and studying how it compares to standard Empirical Risk Minimization.
* **Brown University** — *Aug. 2024 – May 2025.* Collaborated with Prof. Randall Balestriero on self-supervised learning for anomaly detection. This work earned an **oral presentation and best paper award** at the LatinX in AI Workshop (NeurIPS 2024).
* **Cooper open-source project** — *2022.* Contributed a state-of-the-art algorithm to a library for constrained optimization in PyTorch, validating it by reproducing the original experiments.

Engineering
======
As an AI/ML Engineer at **EXIT83 Consulting** (2023–2025) I trained a defect-detection model on 2.3M frames curated from 25,000+ sewer inspection videos, cut training cost by 70% with FFCV and low-priority Azure clusters, and deployed it on AWS. The deployed system sped up inspector throughput by 7x and improved their accuracy by 20%. I also built agentic AI systems for multi-step research and data-synthesis workflows, and a low-compute gesture recognition network for Time-of-Flight sensors on airplane entertainment systems.
