I am two weeks away from starting the second year of my Master's in AI, and I wanted to make a post sharing my experiences in the past year.

To start, it has always been my dream to do my Master's abroad. I knew it since the beginning of my Bachelor's. As a person who grew up in South America, it was difficult to be accepted at any institution that held up to my strict standards. So after nearly five years of hard work, I was super excited to get into my top choice, UvA.

The thing that I liked the most about this university was their research community. UvA has had a crucial role in advancing the field of AI from the very beginning, and I was eager to be part of it. So the first thing that I did after getting accepted was to email a professor, for which I (eventually) got a response.

When we met, he told me that the first year of the Master's was time consuming, and that it would be very hard to maintain a project on the side. I insisted, trusting in my previous research experience. Maybe other students would find it hard to do this, but I could definitely do it. And just like that, I was paired with a PhD and was tasked with the ambitious project to reproduce a recent paper. Spoiler alert: I couldn't do it.

It turns out the professor was right, it was just too much. Aside from school, I was living very far from home, alone, for the first time ever. This wasn't as dramatic as it sounds, but it definitely made things harder. Long story short, after four months, I admitted to myself that this was too ambitious and I would rather just focus on my courses.

From the very beginning of my Master's, I thought that my courses were AMAZING. They were all challenging and incredibly insightful. They did take most of my time, and I very much enjoyed that they did. And to my luck, some of them were actually project courses, which taught me A LOT about research and group work. Anyways, this is the last time I will be taking courses like this and I wanted to get the most out of it. So I let myself be a full-time student one last time, and a year passed by.

Now, my second year is starting, and I am about to start a project with a member of one of my favorite labs at UvA, AMLab. I'll be working on a new approach for non-autoregressive ASR using continuous flow-based models. I am also very happy to share that I am well on track for ELLIS and graduating with honours, two goals that I set for myself at the beginning of the programme.

Finally, I spent the whole summer working on modernizing DL1's coding homeworks, and in September, I'll start working as a TA for ML1. I'll be teaching 4h of class every week and assisting the course throughout the period. It really does get better in the second year ;).

This is a very compressed story about how I spent my last year, although it was slightly longer than I intended. I will be posting more about my journey throughout this year, whenever I have the time. I hope you enjoyed it.
